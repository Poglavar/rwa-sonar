Reading a prospectus once is not research; a term that changes after you read it is the finding. So every document, API endpoint and on-chain account the rest of this site relies on is registered here and re-read on its own schedule: the prospectuses, terms and disclosures daily, the extension state and the authority keys of every mint hourly, the market snapshots diffed day against day. When something moves — a redemption rail, a governing law, a treasury balance, a venue, a paused flag, a rotated mint authority — it becomes an event in the feed below with the value before, the value after, and what it was read from. And every structured fact on a dossier is a claim: the source's own words, the page or clause they sit on, and three timestamps — when we recorded it, when a watcher last looked, and when the quote was last found verbatim.

What changed in the real world

A readable record of issuer, venue, protocol and source changes, plus exact mints entering or leaving our catalogue. “Entered” means we first had enough evidence to catalogue that Solana mint; it does not mean the token was issued that day. Our own research corrections and transient collection failures are not published here.

    Sources we watch

    One row per URL we rely on, keyed by the hash of the URL, which is also the folder its fetched copies live in. A fetch that comes back byte-identical only moves last checked; a fetch with new text writes a version, diffs it against the previous text and can raise a change event. A blocked or missing document is a state, not a silence — a host that refuses us (blocked) and a document that has gone (gone) are both counted here rather than quietly dropped, because "we could not read it" must never look like "nothing to report".

      Detailed observation feed

      Newest first. The severity is the cheapest signal that fired: a lost claim quote is a warning with no model involved, a diff touching redemption, custody, governing law, freeze, pause, clawback, authority or eligibility is a caution, and anything softer is information. Every row carries the evidence it was read from — the source version for a document diff, the account and slot for an on-chain change, the two snapshot dates for a market move.

      Since

        Evidence freshness, issuer by issuer

        How much of what we say about an issuer is the source's own words. Confirmed means the quote was found verbatim in the document; inference means it is our reading rather than anyone's words; unverified that it is recorded but not yet found verbatim — and an unverified claim is never counted as a confirmed one. A claim whose quote later disappears becomes changed, not false: a human decides. Click an issuer to open its dossier.

          Look up a claim

          Pick an issuer and, if you like, type part of a field path (redemption, custody, fees). The field filter matches here in the page rather than in SQL, because the API's field parameter is an exact match and nobody knows a dotted field path by heart.
