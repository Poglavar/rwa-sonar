How to read these charts

What “tokenized stock” leaves unsaid

Four different questions, not one score

A liquid token can be a weak legal claim. A tightly controlled token may be recoverable after theft but unsuitable as final DeFi collateral. We keep those trade-offs separate.

01

Market

Liquidity, reference prices, holders, spreads and observed trading.

 02

Control

Mint, freeze, clawback, pause, allowlist and upgrade powers.

 03

Legal & evidence

Who owes what to whom, in which jurisdiction, supported by which documents.

 04

DeFi use

Confirmed protocol support, collateral terms and what custody inside a contract means.
