Who can freeze, move or change your tokens

A tokenized stock sits in your wallet, but the issuer usually keeps powers over it: to mint more, to freeze your account, to move or burn your balance, to pause every transfer, to restate what your balance displays, to charge a fee on each transfer, or to change the program that does all of this. This page checks the same seven powers for every programme and says who holds each one: a single key anyone with the key file can use, a multisig that needs several signers, a program controlled in practice by whoever holds its upgrade key, nobody (the power is not installed), or unknown when the research has not established it. Holding a power does not mean the issuer is allowed to use it; what the documents allow is in each issuer's dossier. Tap a cell for the exact addresses and the evidence.
