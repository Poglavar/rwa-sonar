## Below the surface

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: sophisticated editorial brand illustration for RWA Sonar, tokenized-asset research; landing-page explainer and pitch visual.
Primary request: show an elegant blue-white iceberg in ocean cross section: a small bright tip above a very clear waterline and a much larger intricate submerged body, while one small quietly smiling blue dolphin detective with a simple cobalt headlamp swims beside the underwater body, shining a gentle cone of light onto a facet. The visual allegory is examining hidden complexity, not danger or treasure.
Style/medium: refined ink and gouache editorial illustration, carefully composed cut-paper-like angular ice planes with very subtle grain; sophisticated adult research publication, no glossy cartoon or photorealism. Dolphin has a streamlined recognizable silhouette, tiny kind eye, modest closed-mouth smile, simple dark-blue lamp strap, natural fins, no hands or clothing.
Composition: landscape 3:2 canvas. Whole iceberg and dolphin visible, composed as one sparse centered vignette, substantial blank margin; waterline runs horizontally through upper third. Underwater scene is a contained pale translucent blue wash with a few graceful contour lines, fades cleanly toward page-colored edges. No hard border. Exaggerated submerged size is symbolic, not a quantitative diagram.
Color palette: off-white #f7f5f0 page background, ice white, pale mist blue, cobalt #3154d8, navy #172033. Fine dark blue outlines, restrained pale lamp beam. Air above water remains mostly empty.
Constraints: no written labels, letters, numbers, charts, embedded financial symbols, buildings, treasure, badges, title, watermark, scary darkness, childish big eyes, multiple dolphins. Art only; labels will be HTML outside the image.
```
