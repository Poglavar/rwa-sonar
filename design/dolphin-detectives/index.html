Usage rules

Rules for using the illustrations

  • Keep the sonar mark. The dolphin is a research companion; the existing logo stays.
  • Protect serious moments. No smiling mascots beside theft, insolvency, missing backing or liquidation warnings.
  • Preserve readable data. No art behind tables, charts, evidence or comparison values.
  • Keep the scene still. No animation or floating assistant. Full compositions and smaller delivery images work on phones.
  • Separate collection from review. The detectives represent watchers and research tools. Every legal conclusion still needs evidence and a recorded review.
  • The iceberg is only an illustration. Its proportions are not a measured risk distribution.
