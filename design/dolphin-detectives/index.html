The restraint is part of the identity

Charm around the research.
Not around the verdict.

  • Keep the sonar mark. The dolphin is a companion, not a new logo on every page.
  • Protect serious moments. No smiling mascots beside theft, insolvency, missing backing or liquidation warnings.
  • Preserve readable data. No art behind tables, charts, evidence or comparison values.
  • Make motion optional. This first set is completely still. Any later motion must respect reduced-motion preferences.
  • Separate collection from review. The detectives are an analogy for watchers and research tools, not a claim of autonomous legal verification.
  • Keep the metaphor honest. The iceberg is illustrative; its proportions are not a measured risk distribution.
