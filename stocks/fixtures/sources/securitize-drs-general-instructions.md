# How Investors Can Tokenize Their Existing Equity Shares

## 1. Determine How Your Shares Are Held

### • Shares Held at a Broker

Most investors hold shares through a brokerage account. You still own the shares, but the broker holds them “in street name” on your behalf. To move shares from your broker to the transfer agent for tokenization, you will need to follow the instructions outlined below to DRS or DWAC your shares.

### • Shares Already Registered with the Transfer Agent

If your shares are already registered directly in your name with the transfer agent, they are likely already in book-entry (DRS) form. In this case, you can skip the broker process and proceed directly to **Step 5: Tokenizing Your Shares.**

## 2. Requesting Direct Registration

To move shares to your own name at the transfer agent, you will need to request your broker to transfer them through the Direct Registration System (DRS) or Deposit and Withdrawal at Custodian (DWAC) method. The process changes from broker to broker (physical vs electronic forms, online request, etc) so you may have to explore the broker’s website or contact the support team at the broker to understand their exact process.

- Not all brokers support DRS transfers. If your broker does not, you may:
  - Move your shares to a broker that supports DRS,
  - Ask if a DWAC can be completed as an alternative, or
  - Acquire the tokenized asset directly through the [Securitize Trading Portal](/).
- Fees, if any, can vary broker to broker.

## 3. The Broker Sends the Request

Your broker will send the transfer instructions to the company’s transfer agent, the firm responsible for maintaining the official record of shareholders.

- Once the request is submitted, the broker will update you on progress and you will see your shares leave your broker account upon completion.

## 4. Transfer Agent Creates Your Account

Once the transfer is verified, the transfer agent records you as the registered owner of the shares.

- You will receive an account statement or confirmation showing your name, account number, and the number of shares you hold.
- After the transfer, you can view and manage your holdings directly with the transfer agent, often through an online portal.
