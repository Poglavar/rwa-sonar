2025088487601 From June 30, 2020, to May 10, 2024, when recommending that his retail customers sell Class A mutual funds and then purchase new Class A mutual funds that imposed a new front-end sales charge, Brode failed to consider whether his customers could have achieved their objectives by exchanging into a new mutual fund within the same fund family, which would not have required the customers to pay a new front-end sales charge. As a result, Brode willfully violated the Care Obligation of Rule 15/-1 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (Reg BI) and violated FINRA Rule 2010. Brode is suspended for two months from associating with any FINRA member in all capacities and fined $5,000. AWCs (Letters of Acceptance, Waiver, and Consent)

2023077020901 Between January 2018 and April 2024, Haywood failed to accurately compute its required customer reserve, as required by Section 15(c)(3) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 and Exchange Act Rule 15c3-3, resulting in at least 36 hindsight deficiencies. The firm's inaccurate customer reserve computations also caused the firm to maintain inaccurate books and records. As a result, Haywood violated Sections 15(c)(3) and 17(a) of the Exchange Act, Exchange Act Rules 15c3-3, 17a-3, and 17a-4, and FINRA Rules 4511 and 2010. During this same period, Haywood failed to establish and maintain a supervisory system, including written supervisory procedures (WSPs), reasonably designed to achieve compliance with the customer reserve requirements of Exchange Act § 15(c)(3) and Exchange Act Rule 15c3-3. As a result, Haywood violated FINRA Rules 3110 and 2010. For these violations, Haywood is censured and fined $275,000. AWCs (Letters of Acceptance, Waiver, and Consent)
