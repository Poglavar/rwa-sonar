Creations, redemptions and the real float

Like ETF flows, but read off the chain: how many tokenized shares each issuer created and redeemed on Solana each day, in dollars, and how much of an xStock's supply the public actually holds. Every figure comes from a transaction or account we read, with the time we read it. A day we did not read is shown as missing, never as zero; a day we read partly says how many hours; a dollar total with units nothing could price is marked as a lower bound.

xStocks: supply is not float

Redeemed xStocks are not burned. The Base Prospectus defines de-activation as a transfer back to the wallet the Tokenizer holds for the Issuer, so redeemed tokens sit in issuer inventory and still count in the on-chain supply. Public float = mint supply − balances of the issuer-attributed wallets, read in raw units in one pass.
