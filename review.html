    How priorities work

    P0 — verify before relying

    A consequential ownership, insolvency, redemption, control or DeFi source changed or disappeared.

    P1 — material gap

    A required consequential field is missing, conflicting or unsupported, or an enforcement question remains open.

    P2 — scheduled research

    Evidence is stale, or a less consequential required field still needs primary support.

    P3 — monitor

    Lower-severity events remain visible until an editor reviews and acknowledges them.

    This queue prioritizes research; it is not itself a legal conclusion. An item disappears only when the underlying dossier is corrected, the evidence is renewed, or a watcher event is explicitly acknowledged.
