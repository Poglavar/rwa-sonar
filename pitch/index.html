CLAIM ≠ REALITY

01

The problem

Let ownership move.
Keep custody where it works.

Tokenization can separate the transferable record of ownership or a claim from possession of the underlying asset—making trade, settlement and programmable use easier.

CustodyThe share stays with a custodian

Safekeeping and the official asset register remain institutionally operated.

token transfer
Ownership / claimThe ledger entitlement changes hands

A portable transfer rail can settle and plug into software without moving the underlying each time.

The catch: possession of the token transfers enforceable rights only if the legal documents, register and intermediaries recognise it. That mapping is what RWA Sonar tests.
